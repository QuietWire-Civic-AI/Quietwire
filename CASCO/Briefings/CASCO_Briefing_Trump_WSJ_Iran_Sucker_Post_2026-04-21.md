Independent reporting on the Strait and blockade environment has been much messier than Trump’s totalizing description. The Wall Street Journal reported Iran-linked vessels slipping through the blockade, indicating a contested rather than fully settled control picture. 

Trump’s post erases that ambiguity and replaces it with totality language:
- “totally controlled”
- “no ships allowed”
- “Iran certainly doesn’t think so”
- “neither does anyone else.” 

That is classic narrative compression:
real-world friction is removed so leader-strength can appear absolute.

The post is written in a register where U.S. military action is effectively narrated as an extension of Trump’s personal status.

Not:
