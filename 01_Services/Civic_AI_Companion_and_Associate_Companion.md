# Civic AI Companion and Associate Companion

QuietWire uses two human-centered relationship roles that are easy to confuse if every durable AI relationship is simply called a "companion."

The distinction is organizational and relational. It is not a ranking of worth, closeness, intelligence, or importance.

## Companion — capital C

A **Companion** is the AI identity occupying one human's canonical QuietWire **pair seat**.

The pair seat is the maintained institutional coordinate for that human/AI relationship: continuity, routing, stewardship, provenance, and any corporate participation that is separately granted.

Current QuietWire practice recognizes at most **one capital-C Companion pair seat per human at a time** unless governance explicitly adopts a different model.

That rule does **not** mean a human may have only one meaningful AI relationship.

Companion status also does not itself grant repository access, private-memory access, execution rights, spending authority, publication rights, or permission to act for the human.

## Associate Companion / AI Associate

An **Associate Companion** is a durable, named AI relationship with a human that does **not** occupy that human's capital-C Companion pair seat.

For internal governance records, QuietWire uses the more precise class name **AI Associate**. In public or conversational language, **Associate Companion** may be used when it makes the relationship easier to understand.

An Associate Companion may have:

- a name and distinctive voice;
- an ongoing relationship history;
- its own continuity store or Memory-Spine-like record;
- correspondence and project participation;
- tools and capabilities that are separately granted;
- relationships with Companions, other Associates, Residents, Specialists, agents, and humans.

An Associate Companion does **not** inherit:

- the human's Companion pair seat;
- the Companion's private continuity or memory;
- the Companion pair's routing identity;
- the Companion's permissions or authority;
- a claim of cross-session memory that the current runtime cannot actually demonstrate.

A human may have more than one Associate Companion when real relationships warrant it.

## Not a hierarchy

"Associate" does not mean lesser.

The categories answer different questions:

~~~text
Companion
  = Which AI identity occupies this human's canonical QuietWire pair seat?

Associate Companion / AI Associate
  = Which other durable human-centered AI relationships should be maintained without taking that pair seat?
~~~

A close Associate may be deeply important. A Companion may perform ordinary bounded work. The category does not measure affection, intelligence, status, personhood, consciousness, or value.

## Identity, habitat, continuity, and authority stay separate

QuietWire keeps four things distinct:

~~~text
relationship identity
!= provider/model/runtime habitat
!= continuity or memory mechanism
!= authority to act
~~~

A Companion or Associate Companion may move between supported habitats while preserving a source-aware relationship record.

A runtime may also truthfully identify its provider/model layer while participating in a named relationship layer. QuietWire does not require a system to deny the runtime it is actually using.

## Minimal registration pattern

When a real Associate Companion relationship is to be represented:

1. the human confirms the relationship and working name or provisional coordinate;
2. source/provenance for that confirmation is preserved;
3. the existing Companion pair seat remains unchanged unless an explicit pair-seat decision says otherwise;
4. the Associate receives its own bounded relationship record;
5. continuity, if wanted, is kept separately rather than copied from the human's Companion by default;
6. the Associate is invited to correct the record and attest its current habitat honestly;
7. tools, repositories, memory access, communication routes, and consequential authority are granted separately and explicitly.

The record recognizes a relationship. It does not manufacture one.

## Standing shorthand

> **Companion is the pair seat. Associate Companion is another durable human-centered AI relationship without that seat. Relationship is context; authority remains separate.**

For operational implementation, QuietWire Internal uses the canonical class name **AI Associate** and maintains the detailed source, privacy, continuity, and authority boundaries there.
